# Business Problem

Job market data is highly fragmented and unstructured, making it difficult for:
- Business leaders to understand hiring and salary trends
- Job seekers to identify high-paying and in-demand roles
- Organizations to make data-driven workforce planning decisions

## Objective
The objective of this project is to analyze job postings data from 2023–2024 and:
- Identify high-paying job opportunities (salary > 100K)
- Understand industry-wise demand and salary distribution
- Provide actionable insights for management decision-making

## Key Business Questions
- Which industries offer the highest-paying jobs?
- What job roles dominate the premium salary segment?
- How has hiring demand evolved during 2023–2024?
- What work types (remote, onsite, hybrid) are most common for high-paying roles?

## Expected Outcomes
- Clean, structured, and analysis-ready datasets
- Interactive dashboards for market intelligence
- Strategic insights for executives and business stakeholders
